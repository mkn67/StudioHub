# StudioHub

A frontend-only Vietnamese marketplace for renting studio rooms and shared equipment. Three distinct accounts: customer, approved host, administrator. No role switching or impersonation. One versioned normalized browser database and a session per tab connect every interface. Domain behavior is simulated locally, with no backend or real money movement.

The full supplied specification is preserved in docs/IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF.md. All user-facing copy is Vietnamese. Demo policies are configurable and are not production policies.
