# StudioHub
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. Local frontend with mock repositories; no backend or real payment provider.
## Users
Vietnamese-speaking creators book physical studio rooms. Hosts operate inventory, schedules, bookings and collections. Admins approve Hosts, moderate inventory and resolve exceptions.
## Product Purpose
Discover a specific physical room, inspect availability and policies, rent equipment, pay a simulated deposit and follow the booking through actual checkout.
## Operating Context
Host → Studio → Room Type → physical Room Unit. Shared inventory and policies; persistent, single-browser demonstration data across roles. Time is centralized and controllable.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Deposit precedes Host approval. Booking, payment, refunds and receivables are independent. Approved specification in docs/references takes precedence over presentation guidance. No real authentication security, payment processing, database or concurrency guarantees. No invented tax, damage or commercial policies.
## Brand Commitments
User-approved Cool Neo-Brutalism × Editorial Marketplace × Layered Motion: bright cool canvas, Ocean Cyan primary, supporting blue, white surfaces and dark ink typography/frames. Public editorial surfaces retain sharp geometry, visible borders and selective hard offset shadows. Soft elevation and controlled frost support utility layers; operational data stays calm and opaque. Preserve Bricolage/DM Mono, meaningful category pictograms, clean Vietnamese lettering and natural photography. Business Policy VI v1.1 remains authoritative; all product behavior and layouts stay unchanged. Root DESIGN.md is the sole active visual authority.
## Evidence on Hand
Both original documents preserved byte-for-byte in docs/references. All inventory, prices, people and operating metrics are illustrative fixtures. Stock photography illustrates spaces, not verified inventory.
## Product Principles
Choose exact physical inventory. Show costs and policy before commitment. Keep operational and financial lifecycles independent. Preserve a shared audit trail. Separate demo tools from customer actions.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard operation, labeled forms, native modal focus management, visible focus, reduced motion, mobile layouts at 360–1440px.
